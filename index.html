03 — Less busywork, earlier signals
A1

Renewal Intelligence

Forecasts each account's likelihood to renew or churn two quarters ahead, so CSMs can plan to keep the logo.

A2

Call Summarizer

Summarizes customer calls and keeps internal counterparts informed with no manual effort.

A3

Customer Talk Track

Scans the calendar for upcoming customer calls and prepares a value-driven talk track for each.

A4

Account Intel

Watches public news on every account for expansion signals (launches, acquisitions, funding) and threat signals (layoffs, lost funding).

A5

Churn Analysis

Looks back at churned accounts each quarter with L1, L2 and L3 root-cause analysis, so the team learns for the next one.

A6

Account Usage

Turns feature-level usage data into a plain-language story with a red, yellow or green rating, so threat signals don't go unnoticed.
